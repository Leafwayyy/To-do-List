FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Quick, honest answers to the things people hesitate on most before signing up.

Is it actually free?

Yes. Sign up with Google or an email and password, no credit card, no trial that quietly expires.

Is my data safe?

Your account and tasks are backed by Firebase Authentication and Firestore, with access rules enforced on the server, not just hidden in the app. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It installs like a real app (no app store needed) and keeps working even if you lose signal, syncing back up once you're online.

What if I'm working with other people, not just myself?

That's what Groups are for: a shared workspace with its own roles, an invite system, and a leaderboard, separate from your own personal list.
