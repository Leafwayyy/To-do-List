Privacy Policy

Last updated September 10, 2026

What this covers

This policy explains what the To-Do List app collects, why, and what control you have over it. It applies to the main task list and the shared Group workspace.

What we collect

DataWhere it comes from
Email addressWhen you sign in with Google or create an account with email and password
Display name and profile photoFrom your Google account, or a name you set yourself for group display
Task contentEverything you type into a task: text, subtasks, deadlines, priority, difficulty, effort estimate
Group dataGroup names, invite codes, membership, task comments, and task suggestions if you use the Group workspace
Brain Dump chat (Dusty)What you type, and any image, PDF, or text file you attach, if you use the Brain Dump chat

Local preferencesStored only on your device: auto-sort setting, popup alert setting, activity history for the streak chart, onboarding progress, and your recent Brain Dump chat text (see AI features below)

Where it's stored

Your account and task data are stored in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, both Google Cloud services. We don't run our own separate servers or databases outside of Firebase. Some preferences (like whether auto-sort is on) live only in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

Cookies

We don't set or use any cookies of our own. Staying signed in works through your browser's local storage and IndexedDB instead (the same on-device storage already mentioned above), not a cookie. The only place a cookie could be involved at all is if you sign in with Google: that sign-in popup runs on Google's own domain, and anything it sets there is Google's cookie, under Google's own privacy policy, not something this app reads, sets, or controls. We also don't use any tracking or advertising cookies, ours or anyone else's.

AI features (Brain Dump / Dusty)

The Brain Dump chat (Dusty, the assistant you can open from the app) sends what you type, plus any image, PDF, or text file you attach, to Google's Gemini API to turn it into proposed tasks. This goes through a small server (a Cloudflare Worker) that we run ourselves, which checks that you're a real signed-in user of this app before forwarding anything, and does not store your message or attachment anywhere - it's passed through to Gemini and back, and forgotten. Chat history stays on your own device only, in your browser's local storage, so it survives a reload or switching between the main app and Group instead of disappearing - never sent anywhere except back to us as part of your very next message, the same as everything else on this page. It clears automatically after a few days of not chatting, or any time from your browser's own site settings.

Each time you chat with Dusty, he's also given a read-only snapshot of your current task list, both your personal list and every group you're in, so he can give relevant help and avoid suggesting things you're already tracking. Nothing about this snapshot is stored anywhere beyond that one request.

Dusty can also propose a change to a task you already have, for example updating its priority, difficulty, deadline, schedule, its own text, its steps, or marking it done, if you ask him to. Like everything else he drafts, a proposed edit is only ever applied once you review and confirm it - he never changes a task on his own.

If you ask Dusty to suggest a task to a teammate or post a comment on one of their tasks, and you confirm it, it's posted under your own account exactly as if you'd typed it yourself - see "Who else sees it" below.

Dusty may also remember a short fact about you between chats (a preference, a constraint, something that keeps coming up). He can save one automatically when he's confident it's useful, without asking first - each one is shown to you right in the chat as it's saved, with an immediate one-tap way to remove it if it's wrong or you'd rather he not keep it. Either way, it's never shown to anyone but you, is stored in your own account data in Cloud Firestore, and you can view and delete anything he's remembered at any time from Settings - included if you export or delete your account.

Settings also lets you paste a longer piece of text or upload a file specifically to have Dusty pull out multiple facts at once, instead of one at a time - it goes through the exact same Gemini pipeline described above, and you review and pick which of the results to actually save before anything is written.

Who else sees it

Google Cloud (Firebase) as our hosting and database provider, and Google's Gemini API for the Brain Dump chat described above. We do not sell your data, and we do not currently run any analytics or ad tracking. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

If you join a Group, the tasks, comments, and suggestions you post there, including anything you ask Dusty to send or post on your behalf and then confirm, are visible to the other members of that specific group, that's the point of a shared workspace, but never to anyone outside it. Your current streak and earned achievement badges (not your task history, email, or name) are also kept in a small separate record built specifically to be readable this way, so a group's roster can show them next to your name - the app only ever displays this inside a group you share, though technically it's not restricted to only people you're grouped with the way the rest of your account data is.

Feedback you submit from Settings → Feedback is only ever visible to you and to us (the developer), never to other users, and is used solely to read, triage, and follow up on what you sent.

Your choices and rights

  • Access: everything tied to your account is visible to you directly in the app.
  • Export: you can export your tasks and anything Dusty has remembered from Settings at any time.
  • Deletion: you can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Account. This removes your profile, your tasks, and your membership in any groups. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.
  • Correction: you can edit your display name and any task content at any time.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes it right away, we don't keep backups beyond what's needed for a short operational window.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone in that age group.

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
